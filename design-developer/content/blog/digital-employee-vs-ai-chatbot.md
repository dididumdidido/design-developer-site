Everyone is selling AI right now.

Chatbots. Agents. Assistants. Copilots.

And if you're like me, you've tried a few. You set them up. You prompted them. You checked their work. And somehow you ended up with MORE on your plate, not less.

So let's clear this up. Because a digital employee is not a chatbot with a new name.

## A chatbot reads a script

A chatbot answers questions. That's the job.

Someone asks something, it replies. Usually from a script or a knowledge base. When the conversation ends, so does its work.

Nothing wrong with that. But it doesn't move anything forward on its own. Someone still has to take what it collected and do something with it.

That someone is usually you.

## A digital employee works the queue

A digital employee is built to finish a piece of work. End to end.

A new lead comes in. It replies in seconds. It qualifies them. It books the call. It updates your CRM. It tells you what happened.

That's the difference. One answers. The other completes.

## Hire it like a person

The easiest way to think about it: you don't BUY a digital employee. You HIRE one.

So you give it what you'd give any new hire:

- **A job description.** What it owns, and what it doesn't touch.
- **KPIs.** How you'll know it's doing the job. Response time. Calls booked. Articles published.
- **A seat on your org board.** Who it reports to, and who it works next to.
- **A review.** You check its numbers weekly, like you would with anyone on your team.

A chatbot doesn't have a KPI. A digital employee is built around one.

## A quick way to tell them apart

Ask the vendor three questions:

1. **What does it own from start to finish?** If the answer is "it answers questions," it's a chatbot.
2. **What KPI is it accountable for?** If there isn't one, you'll be the one tracking it.
3. **Who fixes it when it breaks?** If the answer is you, plan for that time.

## AI as support, not replacement

Here's the part I care about most.

A digital employee isn't there to replace your people. It's there to take the work that keeps getting pushed aside by the urgent, so you and your team can do the human work. Leading. Creating. Serving.

That's the whole point. Less on your plate. More time for the work only you can do.
